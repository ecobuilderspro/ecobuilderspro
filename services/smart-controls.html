Smart Controls & Energy Management

Smart controls and energy management systems use real-time data to manage loads and coordinate solar, storage, and EV charging. EcoBuilders installs and integrates these systems so sites respond intelligently to changing conditions. Edge-based systems act as a local hub, communicating over Modbus, OCPP, and other TCP protocols so equipment can share data and respond together.
